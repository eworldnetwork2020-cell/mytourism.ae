Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Everything you need to know about our tours & transfers

How do I book your ride or luxury chauffeur service with MY Tourism?
You can book your ride instantly through our quick inquiry form on the website or connect directly with our team via WhatsApp at +971 561402248.
What is included in the Dubai Desert Safari packages?
Our desert safari packages typically include red dune bashing, camel riding, sandboarding, live entertainment shows, and an international BBQ buffet dinner, with options to include a quad bike ride.
Are petrol, Salik, and insurance included in luxury car and chauffeur rentals?
Yes! For our hourly luxury fleet rentals (such as the Lexus 300H, Mercedes, and vans/buses), the price includes comprehensive insurance, petrol, Salik (toll gates), parking, Wi-Fi, and bottled water.
Do you provide airport transfer services at Dubai International Airport (DXB)?
Yes, we offer 24/7 airport transfer services with professional meet-and-greet options across Dubai, Abu Dhabi, and Sharjah airports using our entire luxury fleet.
Is there an extra surcharge for trips outside Dubai, such as to Abu Dhabi?
Yes, inter-emirates transfers to Abu Dhabi or other locations outside Dubai incur a standard extra surcharge ranging from AED 100 to AED 300 depending on the specific vehicle type.
